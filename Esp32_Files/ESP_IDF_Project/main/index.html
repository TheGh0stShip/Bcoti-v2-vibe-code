At your right-eye 9 o'clock mounting position, X/Y may look diagonal or reversed. Tap X + once while watching a warm target, then X − to undo; repeat for Y. Keep the direction that improves the overlay. Each change applies and saves automatically.

Image orientation and adjustment limits

Digital alignment needs camera flip OFF. If your OLED driver supports mirroring, hold brightness + about 4 seconds while on to toggle X mirroring; hold it during power-on until orientation changes to toggle Y. These are display controls, separate from this page. Check orientation before aligning.

If those controls are unsupported and camera flip is needed, X/Y alignment is unavailable. Software cannot add display mirroring or 90-degree rotation. At an adjustment limit, zoom is not increased. Check seating and available adjustment at your existing mount; the remaining error needs a physical correction.
